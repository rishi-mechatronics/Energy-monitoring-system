# Video Demonstration

**real time calculations.mp4**

Short demo video showing the Energy Consumption Monitoring System in operation. It shows:

- The Arduino Uno sampling live current and voltage readings from the sensors
- Real-time power (P = I × V) and cumulative energy (Wh/kWh) calculations displayed on the LCD screen
- The system switching between the energy consumption screen and the efficiency status screen (Efficient Power / Warning: Near Limit / Limit Exceeded)
- The LED indicator lighting up during active monitoring
